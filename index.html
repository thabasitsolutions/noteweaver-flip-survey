Meet NoteWeaver Flip

Weave · Flip · Remember

NoteWeaver Flip is a digital notebook app designed to bring notes, books, documents and photos together in one organised library. This research survey asks students, teachers and working professionals what they need from an app like this.

▤Books and notesCreate and organise digital learning or work material.
